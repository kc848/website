01Who we are

This website is run by Dhirubhai Estates, a real estate advisory firm at 104, Leena Residency, Gulmohar Road, JVPD Scheme, Juhu, Mumbai 400049, registered with MahaRERA under number [AGENT REGISTRATION NUMBER]. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Dhirubhai Estates.

This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website and our related channels, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it.

02What we collect

We only collect what we need to help you. Depending on how you contact us, this may include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • What you are looking for: the kind of property, area, budget and timing you tell us about
  • Details of a property you want to sell or lease, if you are an owner
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, email, phone or through this website
  • Basic technical information when you visit, such as your device, browser and the pages you view, collected through cookies (see below)

03How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Reply to your enquiry and arrange calls, meetings and viewings
  • Share residences, opportunities and market information that match what you asked for
  • Prepare valuations and present your property, if you are an owner
  • Keep records required by law, including under real estate and tax regulations
  • Understand how the website is used, so we can improve it

04Your consent

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you contact us or submit your details, and where the law otherwise allows it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us. Withdrawing consent will not affect anything we did before you withdrew it, but it may mean we can no longer help with your enquiry.

05Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only when it is needed to help you, and only as much as is needed:

  • With developers, owners or their representatives, when you ask about a specific property or request a viewing
  • With lawyers, banks and other professionals involved in your transaction, with your agreement
  • With service providers who run our website, email, messaging and records for us, under confidentiality obligations
  • With government or regulatory authorities, where the law requires it

06How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we are helping you, and afterwards only for as long as the law requires or as needed to resolve any questions about a transaction. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

07How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. If a personal data breach occurs that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

08Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update it
  • Ask us to delete it, unless we are required to keep it
  • Withdraw your consent
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied with our response, with the Data Protection Board of India

09Cookies

The website uses a small number of cookies to make it work properly and to understand how visitors use it. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies when you first visit, and change your choice at any time in your browser settings.
