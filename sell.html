FOR OWNERS

How we present a home.

A home is shown the way a good hotel shows a suite: carefully, honestly, and only to the right few people. Here is how we sell or lease yours.

  1. 01

    A private valuation

    We visit your home and tell you honestly what it could command, based on what similar homes in your building and street have actually sold or leased for, not on asking prices.

    • One visit, at your convenience
    • Recent transactions in your building and lane
    • A clear recommendation on price and timing
  2. 02

    Presentation

    Your home is photographed, filmed and written about properly, so that the right buyer understands it before they ever walk in.

    • Professional photography and a short film
    • A written story of the home
    • A private brochure, shared selectively
  3. 03

    Introductions, not open houses

    We show your home to qualified buyers and tenants from our own network first, and go wider only if you choose. Every viewing is accompanied.

    • Buyers vetted before they visit
    • Accompanied viewings only
    • Public portals only with your approval
  4. 04

    Negotiation to registration

    We negotiate on your behalf with the market data to support it, then manage the agreement, payments and registration until the deal is fully closed.

    • Offers presented with context
    • Agreement and documentation handled
    • Registration and handover

For owners who are leasing.

The same care, for a tenancy instead of a sale.

  • The right tenant

    We verify every tenant before they are introduced to you.

  • Fair terms

    Rent, deposit and lock-in agreed on your behalf, with the market to back it.

  • Paperwork handled

    The leave and licence agreement, registration and police verification.

  • After move-in

    One point of contact for renewals and anything that comes up.
