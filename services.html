SERVICES

How we can help.

Since 1984 we have advised on homes and buildings across Mumbai's western suburbs. Whether you are buying, selling, leasing or investing, the same person stays with you from the first conversation to registration.

  1. 01

    Buying a home

    We start with how you want to live, not a list of flats. Then we show you a short, considered selection, including homes that are never listed publicly, and handle everything until the keys are yours.

    FOR BUYERS

    • A brief built around your family and budget
    • Private access to off-market homes
    • Accompanied viewings and honest advice
    • Negotiation, legal checks and registration
  2. 02

    Selling a home

    We price a home on what similar homes in your building and street have actually sold for, present it properly, and introduce it to qualified buyers first, quietly and at your discretion.

    FOR OWNERS

    • A private valuation of your home
    • Photography, film and a written presentation
    • Introductions to our own buyer network first
    • Negotiation through to registration
  3. 03

    Leasing

    Premium homes for rent, and the right tenants for owners. We vet both sides, agree terms that protect them, and manage the paperwork from first viewing to move-in.

    FOR OWNERS AND TENANTS

    • Premium residential rentals
    • Tenant and owner verification
    • Terms negotiated on your behalf
    • Leave and licence agreements and registration
  4. 04

    Investment advisory

    For buyers looking at income rather than a home: we find assets with a clear story behind the numbers, and tell you plainly when something does not add up.

    FOR INVESTORS

    • Pre-leased commercial property
    • Rental housing and operator-led assets
    • Early access to new launches
    • Due diligence on title, approvals and tenants
  5. 05

    Commercial

    Offices, retail and ground-floor shops across the western suburbs and beyond, leased or sold with the same care we bring to homes.

    FOR BUSINESSES

    • Office and retail leasing
    • Ground-floor and high-street units
    • Built-to-suit requirements
    • Lease structuring and paperwork
  6. 06

    For developers

    Sales and marketing mandates for new projects, from positioning and pricing to bringing the right buyers through the door, built on four decades of local relationships.

    FOR DEVELOPERS

    • Exclusive and co-exclusive mandates
    • Positioning, pricing and launch strategy
    • Channel partnership
    • Buyer and investor introductions

OUR PROMISE

We advise. We never push.

For forty years, the firm has grown on goodwill: on families who came back, and sent their friends. That only happens when people trust that the advice they get is meant for them.

  1. 01

    Our advice is free.

    Ask us anything about a home, a building or a neighbourhood. A conversation with us costs you nothing and commits you to nothing.

  2. 02

    You decide, in your own time.

    We will never rush you or talk you into a property. If something is not right for you, we will tell you so, even if it means no deal.

  3. 03

    Clear about how we are paid.

    Our only fee is the brokerage agreed with you in writing, and only once you choose to go ahead. No hidden charges, ever.

How we work.

The same four steps, whichever side of the table you are on.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    At our office, at your home, or on a call. We listen first and tell you honestly what is possible.

  2. 02

    A clear plan

    A shortlist for buyers, a valuation for owners, a brief for investors. No long lists, no pressure.

  3. 03

    Viewings and negotiation

    We accompany every viewing and negotiate on your behalf, with the market data to back it up.

  4. 04

    Paperwork to registration

    Legal checks, agreements and registration, handled until the deal is fully closed.
